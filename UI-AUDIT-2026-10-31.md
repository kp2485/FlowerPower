# UI audit, 2026-10-31

An adversarial pass over commit `a71da49`: live attacks on the iOS 27 simulator
(iPhone 17, a day-586 colony) and three independent code reviews of the phone,
watch, widget and Live Activity interface.

How each finding was checked:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LIVE** | Reproduced on the simulator |
| **CODE** | Confirmed by reading the code myself |
| **REVIEW** | Traced by a reviewer, not rechecked by me |
| **MAYBE** | Depends on runtime behaviour nobody has established |

## Status after the fix pass

Every finding below was re-checked against the code, and all 68 have been dealt
with. One was found during verification and added as 69. The list stands as the
record of what was wrong; this section says where each one ended up.

**Fixed in behaviour:** 1–38, 40, 42–49, 51–66, 68, 69.

**Fixed by telling the truth, because the behaviour is a platform limit or a
design choice:**

- **39.** Notifications still depend on when iOS wakes the app. The copy now says
  so instead of promising otherwise.
- **41.** Backups still carry no photographs. Settings and the restore screen now
  say so, and a flower that cannot be sent says why.
- **50.** First-run screens no longer treat the placeholder as a colony.

**Changed from what the finding proposed:**

- **8.** Saying "Seal It" without the propolis keeps the player's word: the bees
  seal as soon as they have enough.
- **15.** "Let Them Go" is now a recorded answer that closes the question. The

- **55.** First run asks, defaulting from the phone's region, because the app has

**69 (new).** "Current workforce" and "Who is doing what" counted who *can* do a

## 1. Crashes and lost colonies

1. **A tampered backup crashes the app on opening.** **LIVE.** A `.flowerhive`
   with honey at 1e300 traps in `Int(cells.rounded(.up))` at
   `Resources.swift:157`, reached from `RestoreBackupView.swift:77` before the
   player confirms anything. The saved colony survived.
2. **A crafted backup can brick the app for good.** **CODE**, not run.
   `SimClock.swift:112` has no upper bound on `tick`, `tick + pending` overflows
   at `SimClock.swift:157`, and `restore` saves at once, so every launch traps
   and the old colony is already gone. `Lineage.swift:124` has the same overflow
   for queen numbers; `SwarmShare` clamps it and `SaveArchive` does not.
3. **Restoring a backup quietly fast-forwards it.** **CODE.** `GameStore.restore`
   keeps the archive's old clock, so the next ticker pass runs the whole gap with
   no report. The screen promises "Day 120" and 20 seconds later it is much
   later, often dead. Restoring to undo a death usually kills the colony again.
4. **Files are read before their size is checked.** **CODE** for the path,
   **MAYBE** for the crash. `ContentView.swift:303,312` calls
   `Data(contentsOf:)` on the main thread ahead of every size limit.
5. **A `.flower` image bomb.** **MAYBE.** The cap is on compressed bytes, and
   `UIImage(data:)` then decodes at full size in `ReceiveFlowerView.swift:87-91`
   and on every garden thumbnail afterwards.
6. **An unreadable save leads to first run, and picking a site overwrites it.**
   **MAYBE.** `GameStore.swift:119-133`, `GamePersistence.swift:180-191`. The
   preserving copy can fail silently, and there is no retry.

## 2. Things that do not work

7. **Opened files can stop working until a relaunch.** **LIVE.** A bad backup,
   then a bad flower, opened close together. UIKit logged "Attempt to present
   PlatformAlertController … which is already presenting", and after that every
   opened file did nothing, with no error. Settings still opened. This is the
   queue added in `a71da49` (`ContentView.swift:102-110,324-348`): the 600 ms
   Task never rechecks that the way is clear, one slot holds the waiting file,
   and `isClearToPresent` knows nothing of child sheets. The exact interleaving
   is not pinned down.
8. **"Keep It Open" never closes the autumn card.** **LIVE.**
   `ColonySnapshot.swift:607` ignores `entranceDecision`. The answer saves, the
   card stays with both buttons, and every tap adds a haptic and an almanac row.
   The watch treats it as answered. **Seal It** with too little propolis does
   nothing visible and also turns off the instinctive sealing
   (`Simulation.swift:969`, `PostureSystem.swift:46`). **CODE.**
9. **A siege cannot be answered while any posture is held.** **CODE.**
   `DecisionViews.swift:55` swaps the buttons for "Holding: Make Room", and the
   widget, watch and notification all drop the decision too. `Simulation.respond`
   would have accepted the answer.
10. **Closing capture during "Looking at it…" still plants the flower.** **CODE.**
    `CaptureView.swift:115-118` starts an unstructured Task that is never
    cancelled, and nothing can delete a flower.
11. **A colony that dies while you are away loses its report and blocks files.**
    **CODE.** The report sheet is on `tabs` (`ContentView.swift:277`), which
    `NewColonyView` has replaced. `pendingReport` stays set, so backups and swarms
    wait until the player founds the colony they were meant to replace.
12. **Tapping a notification's body goes nowhere.** **CODE.** The default action
    reaches `ContentView.swift:138` with a nonce and no branch handles it, so the
    player stays on the tab they were on.
13. **"Not Now" means notifications are gone for good, and Settings says on.**
    **CODE.** Nothing reads the authorisation status or links to system settings;
    the only `requestAuthorization` is `BackgroundRefresh.swift:202`. A new-phone
    restore during first run never reaches the prompt at all.
14. **Moving the hive has no confirmation.** **CODE.** One tap on "Abscond to
    Here" gives up the comb, stores and brood. `NewColonyView.swift:43` always
    preselects Living Tree, even from "Move to a Larger Cavity", so it can be a
    smaller site (`DecisionViews.swift:214`).
15. **Watch "Let Them Go" detaches the wrist for up to 6 hours.** **CODE** for the
    no-op, **REVIEW** for the rest. `DecisionAction.swift:200` reports success and
    changes nothing, so the watch discards phone summaries that still ask.
16. **The watch shows old data as current when reopened out of range.** **CODE.**
    There is no `scenePhase` handling in the watch target. An expired decision
    stays tappable.
17. **Flowers vanish from the map after following a swarm or starting over.**
    **REVIEW.** `gardenRings` resets while patches keep ring 3 cells, so they are
    undrawn and a new flower can land on top (`Simulation.swift:106,409`,
    `WorldView.swift:517,590`).
18. **Begin Again may freeze the dashboard.** **MAYBE.** `startLiveUpdates()` runs
    before `refresh()` at `GameStore.swift:795,514`, so it may read the dead
    snapshot and never start.
19. **Siri or Control Centre on a dead colony opens the camera later.** **CODE.**
    The guard at `ContentView.swift:150` covers `needsSetup`, not `isCollapsed`.
20. **Refused actions look successful.** **REVIEW.** "Open the Nest Up" when it
    cannot be afforded, and siege, split and scout buttons, give a success haptic
    with no change (`ColonyDashboardView.swift:631-646`). Pinning a job with no
    capable bees silently clears the current pin (`JobAssignmentView.swift:116`).
21. **A library pick shows no progress.** **REVIEW.** The chooser stays live during
    `loadTransferable`, so a second capture can start
    (`CaptureView.swift:150-175`).
22. **Limited Photos access defeats the duplicate check.** **REVIEW.** Each pick of
    the same photo adds another patch and camera-roll copy
    (`CaptureView.swift:156,307`).
23. **The relocation sheet vanishes when the swarm resolves.** **REVIEW.**
    `DecisionViews.swift:230`.
24. **Decisions stay live during a long catch-up.** **MAYBE.** No view reads
    `isBusy`, so an answer lands on a weeks-old colony and is thrown away
    (`GameStore.swift:165-179`).
25. **A stale widget or complication after a reset.** **REVIEW.** The widget can
    read "Collapsed" over a living colony for 6 hours
    (`FlowerPowerApp.swift:73-74`), and the complication can show the old colony
    for 4 (`WatchColonyModel.swift:134-187`).
26. **The Live Activity outlives reality.** **REVIEW**; no alive gate confirmed by
    **CODE**. `staleDate` is nil, its "2d" can sit wrong for hours, and it can run
    on a dead colony (`LiveActivities.swift:60-158`).

43. **"Struggling" above "Foraging steadily."** **LIVE.** The status and the

48. **A faded flower says "Out of season".** **REVIEW.** It can show 100% forage

49. **The World header misstates garden growth.** **REVIEW.** The 7th flower opens

50. **First-run copy treats the placeholder as your colony.** **REVIEW.** "Replace

## What held up

- Garbage and empty files give clear errors, and a bad backup says the colony is
  untouched. **LIVE.**
- Map zoom and clamping, cancelling capture, and tab switching. **LIVE.**
- The largest text size wraps body copy cleanly. **LIVE.**
- The crash in finding 1 left the save intact. **LIVE.**
- No normal-play crash was found: no reachable NaN, force unwraps, or bad slider
  ranges. **REVIEW.**
- Privacy claims hold: no location APIs, no `URLSession`, and shared images are
  re-encoded without EXIF. **REVIEW.**
- There are no custom URL schemes, so deep-link attacks do not apply. **CODE.**

Two early alarms were my own test timing, not bugs: a Done button that seemed not
to respond, and a Nest tab that seemed to open capture.
